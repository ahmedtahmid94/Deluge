# Beach Day glossary

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Good conditions | Safe | We never certify safety |
| Rip current risk: check the NWS forecast | Low rip risk | Decision D1: link out, never estimate |
| Tap | Click | Touch devices (HIG) |
| Unable to load the forecast | We're having trouble | HIG: avoid "we" |
| Best 9 am – 1 pm | Optimal window | Plain words |
